about it:
---------
This is are convertions of the Europe in War player models.
The allied model is from IXNAY (ixnay@giga4u.de / www.matze.privat.ms)
and the axis model and some logos are from Res-Q (res_q@gmx.net / www.rescue.privat.ms)

IXNAY: I wanted to make the model like in the movie "James Ryan" so I did, and I placed my
       face on the model, now it looks like me, hahaha.
RES-Q: I converted my old Barney Half-Life Playermodel (SA) into a WiE model, with my face
       like on the Half-Life Playermodel.
